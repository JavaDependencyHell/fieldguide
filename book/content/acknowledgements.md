This book exists because of the generosity and patience of people and organisations who care about getting dependency management right.

### HeroDevs {.unnumbered}

HeroDevs supported the early development of this project. Their focus on securing and maintaining software ecosystems that have reached end-of-life overlaps naturally with the themes explored here. That support gave this book the time and space it needed to take shape. You can learn more about their work at [herodevs.com](https://herodevs.com).

### The Community {.unnumbered}

Thanks to the developers, build engineers, and maintainers who shared war stories, reviewed drafts, and pointed out where the text was wrong. The "Wild" sections in particular owe a debt to the people who've lived through the edge cases the rest of us only read about.

### Tools and Infrastructure {.unnumbered}

Every scenario in this book is backed by a demo project that was built and verified by execution against real tools: Maven, Gradle and sbt on the JVM; pip, Poetry and uv in Python; npm, pnpm and Yarn in Node, with Verdaccio standing in for the registry. Thanks to the teams behind those tools, and to the broader open-source ecosystem that makes this kind of hands-on exploration possible.
